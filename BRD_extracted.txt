Executive Summary LANI Academy requires a holistic digital learning platform that consolidates every training expression of LANI Consulting into one structured, commercial, user-friendly, and scalable ecosystem. The platform must not function merely as a brochure website. It must operate as a full-service digital training infrastructure where: Individual learners can discover open programmes, register, pay, access courses, complete modules, take assessments, and receive certificates. Corporate organisations can explore in-plant, customised, hybrid, virtual, and physical training programmes. Sponsored programmes, internships, bootcamps, accelerators, incubation programmes, and application-based learning programmes can be listed and managed. LANI Academy administrators can upload courses, create cohorts, manage learners, assign tutors, track performance, issue certificates, upload flyers/brochures, and monitor revenue. Sales and commercial teams can track leads, enquiries, corporate requests, conversion pipelines, and training revenue. Learners and clients can experience LANI Academy as a professional, premium, credible, and future-ready learning brand. The platform must represent the full scope of LANI Academy’s training mandate across the eight thematic pillars of LANI Consulting, while also supporting the commercial growth ambition of LANI Academy as a major digital sales and human capital development engine. 2. Its role is to empower individuals and organisations through programmes that support growth, excellence and sustainable success. The Academy has delivered training across Management, Human Capital, Finance, ICT, Fintech, Agriculture and Development, strengthening technical, digital and business skills. Risks and Mitigation Risk Description Mitigation Poor user adoption Learners may not immediately use the platform. Strong launch campaign, course discounts, corporate partnerships and email marketing. Manual content bottleneck Course uploads may delay launch. Create course upload templates and assign content owners. Payment failure Failed transactions may discourage users. Use reliable gateways and allow manual transfer confirmation. Poor LMS experience Learners may abandon courses. Keep UI simple, mobile-first and progress-based. Weak course packaging Courses may not sell if poorly presented. Use strong course pages, flyers, outcomes and testimonials. Certificate credibility risk Certificates must look professional and verifiable. Use branded templates, QR verification and certificate IDs. Corporate lead leakage Enquiries may not be followed up. Route leads to sales dashboard with status tracking. Content inconsistency Different teams may upload uneven content. Use standard course templates and approval workflow. Security risk Learner/payment data may be exposed. Secure hosting, SSL, access control and data protection policies. Scope creep Too many features may delay launch. Implement MVP first, then phased expansion. 24. Final Product Statement The LANI Academy platform should become the central commercial, operational and digital learning infrastructure for all LANI training activities. It should not only display courses; it should enable discovery, registration, payment, learning, assessment, certification, corporate engagement, application management, reporting and repeat sales. The build should therefore be guided by five non-negotiable principles: Commercial first — every course and programme must be positioned to sell. Learner friendly — registration, payment and access must be simple. Corporate ready — HR/L&amp;D teams must be able to request and manage training easily. Content scalable — LANI staff must be able to upload and manage courses without depending on developers every time. Impact measurable — completion, satisfaction, revenue, certificates and corporate outcomes must be tracked. This BRD is detailed enough to guide the website structure, LMS architecture, payment flow, learner dashboard, corporate training portal, application system, certification engine, admin dashboard and creative asset placement.
